## What it shows
- **The lists fixed the failure, partly by more than value matching.** q03 ("active" repos in the "Money" area) went
  from 0/3 covered to 3/3: "Money" is in `platform`'s list, the cue the README expected. q06 ("active" repos that belong
  to no area) went from 0/3 to 3/3 with no value in the question; the model read `platform`'s list as the areas. q12
  ("which area does the repo belong to") is still weak: 1/3 covered, the other two picked `kind` instead. Platform was
  missed 2 times in 12 needed appearances (03: 9 of 12).
- **One new miss, probably noise.** q07 ("what counts as success", needs `check`) was covered 3/3 in 03 and 2/3 here; the
  miss picked `readme`, `note` and `status_note` and not `check`. One call; this run cannot tell a side effect of the
  lists from chance. Total coverage: 57/60 against 51/60; field by field 69/72 against 63/72.
- **The 6-answer gap to `toml` (58 against 52) is half the pick and half reading.** Three answers come from the three
  uncovered picks (q12 ×2, q07 ×1; 0 of 3 right). One is q15 ("active" repos with no give-up condition), wrong in all
  three `picked` and `view` passes and two of three `toml` passes: a reading problem of the question, not of the pick.
  Two are reading slips on picks identical to the hand-fed `view` (q06 once, q18 once), where `view` was right every
  time. Where the pick covered the need, 52 of 57 answers were right (03: 47 of 51).
- **The stop was missed by nothing.** 6 answers behind at n = 60 is exactly 10.0 points; the rule says "more than 10". One
  more wrong answer would have killed it. The verdict follows the rule as written; the margin is not evidence of safety.
- **Against the prediction (README):** coverage 95% (predicted about 93%), accuracy 87% against `toml`'s 97% (predicted
  about 90% against 95%), call 1 1,460 tokens (predicted about 1,420), call 2 2,869 (about 2,690), total 29% (about 27%).
- **Call 1 cost more than the proxy said.** The lists and one sentence add 131 tokens by Claude's count (1,460 against
  1,329); the o200k proxy said +96. The README's +92 came from an earlier wording of the heading; this run's final
  wording is +96 by the same proxy. The proxy runs low on this text, as it did on the data in 01. It passes the
  1,500-token check by 40.
- **Call 2 grew by 182 tokens, because of extra fields.** 10 of 60 picks (17%; 03: 14, 23%) added a field, and these
  were heavier columns than in 03: `note` ×5, `status_note` ×5, `readme` ×4, `stop` ×4, `kind` ×3 (03: `kind` ×5,

  questions (03: 97). `status_note` is a column that has no list, so the lists did not cause it directly; the cause is
  UNKNOWN.
- **Time.** A `picked` question took 13.1 s against 7.0 s for `toml` (medians 13.0 and 6.5), four jobs in parallel,

## Limits
- One wording per field, as in 03. "Area" for `platform` is one word chosen by me; this run says the lists rescued it
  in 7 of the 9 calls that missed it in 03 (q03 and q06 fully, q12 one in three), not that they rescue any word.
- The comparison with 03 is across two runs, same file, same wording, same prompt apart from the lists. A one- or
  two-answer difference is noise: q07's miss and the whole gap to the stop line are inside that.
- 3 of the 20 questions decide the coverage story (q03, q06, q12, nine calls); the accuracy story is 8 wrong answers
  out of 60.
- The lists are bounded by the 12-value rule and cost 131 tokens for 26 values on this file. A table with many
  few-valued columns would pay more; not priced here.
- The two calls are separate `claude -p` processes, each paying the fixed overhead, as in 02 and 03.
